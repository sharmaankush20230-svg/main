farmer-friend
An AI-powered computer vision system to detect plant species and diagnose crop diseases from leaf images. Project: Kisan Mitra (Farmer’s Friend AI Plant Disease Detector)Theme: Science, Technology, and Innovation for Sustainable and Viksit Bharat (Category C1)
What it is: A free web app that works on any smartphone browser without installing software. How it works:The farmer points their phone camera at an infected leaf. The app captures the image and sends it to a cloud vision AI model.Within seconds, it displays the plant name, disease diagnosis, and treatment remedies (both organic and chemical).Key Benefit: Gives farmers early detection of crop blights directly in the field, preventing harvest loss and reducing unnecessary pesticide use.
